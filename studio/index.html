A design studio with its own workshop

Shree Ohm Creation started in Ahmedabad, making furniture by hand. Today we design and build complete homes and offices, still with our own carpenters.

Our story

We began in 2014 as a small furniture workshop in Kankaria, making wardrobes, beds and modular kitchens for families across the east of the city. Word spread, and clients began asking us to design the whole home around the furniture.

That is still how we work. Every project is designed in our studio and made in our own workshop, so the person who drew your kitchen can walk across and check it being built. It keeps quality high, timelines honest and prices fair.

Founder

Ashit Mehta

Ashit Mehta founded Shree Ohm Creation and still leads the studio today. Clients meet Ashit at the first site visit, and Ashit stays involved through design, making and handover.

The idea behind the studio has not changed: listen carefully, build properly, and hand over a home the family is proud of.

The workshop

Built by our own hands in Kankaria

Our workshop has panel saws, edge-banding and spray booths for PU and lacquer, run by carpenters who have been with us for years. Making in-house means we control every joint, every edge and every finish.

  • BWP plywoodWaterproof, for kitchens and wardrobes
  • TeakSolid wood for frames and legs

Process

How we work

  1. Consultation

    We visit your site, measure every room and listen to how you live. Free within Ahmedabad.

    Week 1

  2. Design & 3D

    Layouts, materials and photo-realistic 3D views of each room, revised until they feel right.

    Weeks 2–4

  3. Making

    Furniture is made in our Kankaria workshop while civil, electrical and ceiling work goes on at site.

    Weeks 4–10

  4. Installation

    Our own team fits, finishes and cleans. We walk you through every room at handover.

    Weeks 10–12
