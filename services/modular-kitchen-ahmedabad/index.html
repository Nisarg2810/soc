Modular kitchens in Ahmedabad

L-shaped, parallel, U-shaped and island kitchens, made in our own workshop.

Book a free site visit →

Every kitchen we make is planned around how your family cooks. We measure the room, plan where the hob, sink and fridge go, and design drawers for your actual utensils. Carcasses are made from waterproof BWP plywood in our Kankaria workshop and fitted with soft-close hardware.

An L-shaped modular kitchen in Ahmedabad usually costs between ₹1.8 lakh and ₹4 lakh. Finish, hardware and the number of tall units make the biggest difference.

What's included

  • Layout planning: straight, L, parallel, U or island
  • BWP plywood carcasses, waterproof
  • Laminate, acrylic, PU or veneer shutters
  • Quartz or granite counters
  • Tandem drawers, corner units and tall pantry
  • Chimney, hob and appliance integration
  • Under-cabinet lighting
  • Installation and after-sales service

Questions

Which material is best for a modular kitchen in Ahmedabad?

We recommend BWP (boiling water proof) plywood for carcasses, especially under the sink. It handles water and humidity far better than MDF or particle board.

How long does a modular kitchen take?

About 3 to 5 weeks from final design to installation. Fitting on site takes 3 to 5 days.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Carcasses and workmanship carry our warranty, and hardware carries the manufacturer's warranty.
