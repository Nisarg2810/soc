Home renovation in Ahmedabad

Older homes renovated room by room, handed over ready to live in.

Book a free site visit →

Renovation is harder than a new flat: walls are not straight, wiring is old and families often live at home during the work. We plan the work in phases, handle civil, electrical and plumbing changes, and finish with new furniture so the home is ready to live in.

Renovation costs depend heavily on civil work. We visit, inspect and give a fixed estimate before starting.

Get an estimate

What's included

  • Condition survey and renovation plan
  • Civil changes, tiling and waterproofing
  • New wiring and plumbing
  • Kitchen and bathroom upgrades
  • Wardrobes and furniture
  • Painting and polishing
  • Phased work so you can stay at home
  • One point of contact throughout
Process

How it works

  1. Consultation

    We visit your site, measure every room and listen to how you live. Free within Ahmedabad.

    Week 1

  2. Design & 3D

    Layouts, materials and photo-realistic 3D views of each room, revised until they feel right.

    Weeks 2–4

  3. Making

    Furniture is made in our Vatva workshop while civil, electrical and ceiling work goes on at site.

    Weeks 4–10

  4. Installation

    Our own team fits, finishes and cleans. We walk you through every room at handover.

    Weeks 10–12

Questions

Can we live at home during renovation?

Usually yes. We work room by room and keep one bathroom and the kitchen running for as long as possible.

Do you keep existing furniture?

If you love a piece, we restore it and design around it.
