Before you get in touch

How much does interior design cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on size and materials. As a guide, a complete 3BHK usually costs ₹8 lakh to ₹22 lakh, a modular kitchen ₹1.8 lakh to ₹4 lakh and wardrobes ₹1,100 to ₹2,500 per sq ft. We give a detailed estimate after a free site visit.

Do you make the furniture yourselves?

Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes and furniture are made in our own workshop in Vatva, Ahmedabad, which lets us control quality and timelines.

Which areas of Ahmedabad do you cover?

All of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, including Satellite, Bopal, Shela, Prahlad Nagar, Thaltej, Maninagar, Vatva, Naroda, Gota and Chandkheda.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first site visit and consultation within Ahmedabad are free.
