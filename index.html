How we work

From first visit to handover in about twelve weeks

  1. Consultation

    We visit your site, measure every room and listen to how you live. Free within Ahmedabad.

    Week 1

  2. Design & 3D

    Layouts, materials and photo-realistic 3D views of each room, revised until they feel right.

    Weeks 2–4

  3. Making

    Furniture is made in our Kankaria workshop while civil, electrical and ceiling work goes on at site.

    Weeks 4–10

  4. Installation

    Our own team fits, finishes and cleans. We walk you through every room at handover.

    Weeks 10–12

Questions we're often asked

How much does interior design cost in Ahmedabad?

It depends on size and materials. As a guide, a complete 3BHK usually costs ₹8 lakh to ₹22 lakh, a modular kitchen ₹1.8 lakh to ₹4 lakh and wardrobes ₹1,100 to ₹2,500 per sq ft. We give a detailed estimate after a free site visit.

Do you make the furniture yourselves?

Yes. Kitchens, wardrobes and furniture are made in our own workshop in Kankaria, Ahmedabad, which lets us control quality and timelines.

Which areas of Ahmedabad do you cover?

All of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, including Satellite, Bopal, Shela, Prahlad Nagar, Thaltej, Maninagar, Vatva, Naroda, Gota and Chandkheda.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first site visit and consultation within Ahmedabad are free.

How long does a complete home interior take?

Most 3BHK homes take 8 to 12 weeks after the design is approved.
