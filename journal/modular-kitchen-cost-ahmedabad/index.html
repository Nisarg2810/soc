Modular kitchen cost in Ahmedabad: a 2026 price guide

18 September 20266 min readBy the Shree Ohm Creation team

The most common question we hear is “how much will my kitchen cost?” The honest answer is that it depends on size, layout and finish. This guide gives you realistic 2026 price ranges for Ahmedabad so you can plan before you meet any designer.

Quick answerMost modular kitchens in Ahmedabad cost between ₹1.2 lakh and ₹6 lakh. A typical L-shaped kitchen in a 3BHK flat costs ₹1.8 lakh to ₹4 lakh with BWP plywood, laminate shutters and soft-close hardware.

Price by kitchen layout

LayoutTypical sizeIndicative price
Straight (one wall)8–10 ft₹1.2 – 2.5 lakh
L-shaped10 × 8 ft₹1.8 – 4 lakh
Parallel (galley)2 × 9 ft₹2.2 – 4.5 lakh
U-shaped10 × 8 × 10 ft₹3 – 5.5 lakh
IslandOpen plan₹4 – 7 lakh+

These ranges include carcasses, shutters, counter, standard hardware and installation. Appliances such as the chimney, hob and built-in oven are extra.

Price by shutter finish

The shutter (door) finish is the biggest single choice after layout. Per square foot of kitchen, expect roughly:

  • Laminate: ₹1,200 – 1,800. Durable, easy to clean and the best value.
  • Acrylic: ₹1,800 – 2,800. High gloss and a mirror-like look.
  • PU paint: ₹2,500 – 4,000. Seamless matte or gloss in any colour.
  • Veneer: ₹2,800 – 4,500. Real wood grain, lacquered for protection.

Five things that change the cost

1. Carcass material

BWP (boiling water proof) plywood costs more than MR plywood or MDF, but it is the right choice for the sink area and anywhere near water. Read our plywood guide to see where each grade belongs.

2. Hardware

Tandem drawers, corner carousels and tall pantry pull-outs make a kitchen far easier to use. Branded hardware such as Hettich adds cost but lasts for years.

3. Counter top

Granite is the most affordable. Quartz costs more but resists stains from turmeric and oil, which matters in an Indian kitchen.

4. Number of tall units

Floor-to-ceiling pantry and appliance units add a lot of storage and a lot of material. Each tall unit can add ₹25,000 to ₹60,000.

5. Lighting and accessories

Profile lights, glass shutters and internal organisers are small items that add up. Decide which ones you will use every day.

How to plan your budget

  1. Measure your kitchen and note where the window, gas line and drainage are.
  2. Decide on the layout first, then the finish.
  3. Keep 10% aside for appliances and last-minute changes.
  4. Ask for a line-by-line quote so you can compare like for like.

Prices in this guide are indicative for Ahmedabad in 2026 and vary with brand, design and site conditions. For an exact figure, book a free site visit and we'll measure and quote within a week.
